Design decisions

Night mode of the brand. Burgundy 900 is the sky, the burgundy to plum gradient is the card, lilac marks progress. Cards are drawn like a physical deck, so the physical-card origin carries into the app.

Sheets and the shared-link web page flip to the light cream palette so moments that leave the private space read differently.
