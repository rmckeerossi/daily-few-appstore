Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Daily Few is a private place to reflect. Your answers, voice memos, photos, notes and body check-ins are yours. We never sell them, share them, advertise with them, or use them to train AI.

This policy explains what the Daily Few app and website collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. Daily Few is operated by Onyx Roots, Inc. (“Daily Few”, “we”, “us”).

The short version

  • Your reflections are private. Only you can see your answers, voice memos, photos, entries and monthly notes in the app. We don’t read them.
  • Your body check-ins are private too, and your period days never leave your phone. We never use anything about your health for marketing or advertising.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling. We don’t use advertising or cross-app tracking, and we don’t sell or rent personal information.
  • You’re in control. You can edit or delete any answer, and delete your account and everything in it from Profile, at any time.
  • For adults. Daily Few is for people 18 and older.

Your health information

Your body check-ins and symptoms are health information, and we treat them with extra care:

  • Period days stay on your iPhone. When you mark “Period today”, it’s saved only on your phone. It’s never sent to us or our service providers, so we don’t have it and can’t share it. It’s included in your iPhone’s own backup if you use one, and it’s removed from the phone when you delete your account.
  • Check-ins and symptoms are private to you. They’re stored with your account so they’re there on any phone you sign in on, and access rules on our servers only let your own account read them.
  • Only used for you. We use them only to show you your recap and your patterns. We never use them for advertising or marketing, never include them in any report, never sell or share them, and never use them to train AI.
  • You can delete them. They’re deleted with your account. To have them removed on their own, email us.

Daily Few is for reflection and understanding. It isn’t a medical service, and it doesn’t diagnose or treat anything.

Permissions on your iPhone

  • Photos: only the photos you choose to add. The app can’t browse your library.
  • Microphone: only while you’re recording a voice memo.
  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on. These are scheduled on your phone.
  • Face ID: if you turn on the app lock. Face ID is handled entirely by your iPhone. We never receive any face data.

You can change any of these in your iPhone’s Settings.

Sharing a card

When you share a card, only the question is shared, never your answer. Anyone with a card link can see that one question on our website.

Who helps us run Daily Few

We use a small number of service providers who process information only to provide their service to us, under agreements that protect it:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage and sign-in, in the United States.
  • Resend: sends your sign-in codes by email.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you choose to use it.
  • Sentry: crash reports.
  • Vercel: hosts the web page for shared card links.
  • If you sign up for the Daily Few newsletter or text updates, an email or text messaging provider to send them.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users, and in connection with a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Your reflections, voice memos and photos are stored privately, and access rules on our servers only allow your own account to read them. Access to our systems is limited and protected with two-factor authentication.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete an answer, entry, voice memo or photo, it’s removed. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), your profile and everything you created are permanently deleted straight away. Copies may remain in routine backups for up to 30 days before they’re overwritten. Anonymous usage totals, no longer linked to you, are kept.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information: your details, season and preferences are in Profile.
  • The Daily Few newsletter and text updates: these are off unless you choose them. The newsletter brings new decks, reflections, and news and products from Daily Few. It’s never based on anything you write in the app. You can stop it any time in Profile or with the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Delete: delete any reflection, or your whole account, in the app. Deleting your account also deletes your body check-ins and removes your period days from your phone.
  • Get a copy: email us and we’ll send you a copy of your information.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have additional rights, such as to access, correct, delete or port your information, or to object to certain uses. We honor these requests. Contact us at the address below. We don’t sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

Age requirement

Daily Few is only for people 18 and older. We ask for your birthday at signup and don’t create an account for anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.
