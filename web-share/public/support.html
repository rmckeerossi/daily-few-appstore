We’re here to help.

Something not working, or a question about Daily Few? Write to us and a real person will reply, usually within two business days.

support@dailyfew.com

It helps to include your iPhone model and what you were doing when it happened. Please don’t send us your answers. We never need them to help.

I didn’t get my sign-in code.

Codes come from hello@mail.dailyfew.com and usually arrive within a minute. Check your spam or promotions folder. If it’s been longer, go back and ask for a new code. Each code works for a few minutes.

Who can see my answers?

Only you. Your answers, voice memos, photos and notes are private to your account. When you share a card, only the question is shared, never your answer. You can also lock the app with Face ID in Profile.

How do I change my season of life, reminders or email preferences?

Open Profile, the last icon at the bottom of the app. You can change your season, turn the daily reminder on or off and pick its time, and choose whether you get the Daily Few newsletter or text updates.

I’m not getting reminders.

Make sure notifications are on for Daily Few in your iPhone’s Settings, then turn the daily reminder on in Profile and pick a time.

How do I edit or delete an answer?

Open History, tap the answer, then choose Edit or Delete. Edited answers keep their original date.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile and everything you’ve written, recorded and added, straight away. It can’t be undone.

I’m going through something really hard.

Daily Few is for reflection. It isn’t a substitute for care from a professional. If you’re in crisis in the US, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, any time. If you’re in immediate danger, call 911.
